# QSB Hide And Seek

A addon for QSB that adds Hide And Seek

## Features
- Up to 2 seekers
- All planets (except Echoes of the Eye)
- Grace period
- Planet voting

## Rules
Seekers are randomly picked, they are blinded for 30 seconds, then they are let out and can catch hiders.

Hiders have a 1m grace period after seekers are let out (protects from any deaths except when you get caught)

When a seeker touches a hider, they become a seeker

When a seeker dies, they respawn after 5 seconds in a random spot

You cannot exit the planet because of a invisible barrier, if you somehow make your way past it, you will be teleported to a random point on the planet

After a round is over (everyone has been found), everyone's times will be shown

## FAQ

### How do I start a game?
As a host, the default keybind to start hide & seek is F8, you can rebind this in the mod's config menu

### Are nametags hidden?
Seekers cannot see hiders' nametags, but hiders can see seekers' nametags
This also applies to the map

### Why isn't this mod on OWML?
The OW mod db bans mods with "possible ai usage"

## Installation guide
1. Download latest release ZIP
2. Unzip it at %APPDATA%\Roaming\OuterWildsModManager\OWML\Mods (NOT OWML\\Mods\\Stacik.QSBHideAndSeek)
